# NeroNotes

**Futuristic music for Minecraft — compose your own disks in a dedicated studio dimension, then sync them across your whole base on glowing neon resonator blocks.**

NeroNotes is the **music** mod of the Neroland ecosystem. It replaces the humble note block and jukebox with a family of dark, futuristic **resonant blocks** — matte black with neon glows that pulse when they play — driven by a new wireless **resonance signal** instead of redstone. Compose full tracks in the **Soundforge**, a dedicated creation dimension reached through its own teleporter machine, press them onto custom disks, and broadcast them in perfect sync from every player in your base.

## 1. Resonant blocks & the resonance signal

A new set of programmable sound blocks in the Neroland visual language: deep matte black, edge-lit with neon that flares on every note.

- 🎛️ **Resonant blocks** — tuned percussion, bass, lead and pad blocks with unique futuristic voices (deep bass, high notes, low notes, synth textures).
- 📡 **Resonance signal** — a new wireless trigger mechanic on channels/frequencies, independent of redstone, that fires notes, patterns and whole tracks.
- 🌈 **Neon feedback** — blocks glow, pulse and colour-shift with the music.

## 2. The Soundforge (creation dimension)

Step through the **Harmonic Gate** into a dedicated composition dimension: a quiet, starlit studio space with an oversized sequencer built into the world itself.

- 🎹 **In-world sequencer** — lay out patterns, layers and instruments at your own pace.
- 💾 **Press to disk** — save a finished composition onto a blank disk as a playable custom music disk.
- 🌍 **Share** — mark a composition as downloadable so other players can grab it.

## 3. Custom disks, players & base-wide sync

- 📀 **Custom music disks** — player-made tracks as real items with names and glowing labels.
- 🔊 **Resonators** — the jukebox successors; chain them on a resonance channel and every one plays the **same track in perfect sync**, so one song fills an entire base or station.
- 🛰️ **Disk Exchanger** — a machine to copy, share and download other players' published disks.

## 4. Ecosystem

- **Requires only [Neroland Core](https://github.com/Neroland/neroland-core)** — config, materials, progression and the shared link API.
- Soft synergies: **NeroDecor** (ambience), **NeroEvents** (event stingers), **NeroLink** (browse and share compositions from the companion app). None is required.
